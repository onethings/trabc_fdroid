Trabc is a Traccar GPS tracking client for Android, built with Flutter and OpenStreetMap.

It connects to any Traccar server (self-hosted or one of the public demo servers) and lets you follow vehicles, people and assets in real time.

Features:

* Live tracking on OpenStreetMap-based maps, with no Google services required
* Device list with status, last position and recent history
* Route history playback with speed and stop information
* Geofences: draw, edit and manage circles and polygons
* Reports: trips, stops, summary, events, positions, chart and combined reports
* Notifications and detailed notification settings
* Share devices, send commands, and manage drivers, groups and maintenance
* Offline address lookup with an online fallback
* Light and dark themes, translated into many languages

Trabc contains no proprietary libraries, no ads and no trackers.
